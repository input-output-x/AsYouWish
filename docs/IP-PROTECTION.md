# IP Protection Notes

## Protectable Parts

- Product name and brand expression: AsYouWish / 想你所想.
- Prompt patterns and story format.
- Curated city knowledge data.
- UI flow from arrival to narrated city session.
- Future AR-lite POI story layer.

## Practical Protection

- Keep the repository private until launch strategy is clear.
- Avoid committing API keys, private datasets, or unreleased partner details.
- Keep city knowledge sources documented internally.
- Add copyright notices to public pages before launch.
- Consider trademark screening before heavy public promotion.

## Content Risk

Generated stories may contain inaccurate facts or hallucinated local details. V1 should be positioned as a narrative guide, not an authoritative travel database.

## V1 Public Wording

Use cautious wording:

- "城市叙事"
- "灵感故事"
- "本地线索"

Avoid unsupported claims:

- "最准确"
- "官方"
- "全网最全"
- "实时推荐"
